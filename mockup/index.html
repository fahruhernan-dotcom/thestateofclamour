PARTY RULES & POLICIES

Read carefully before stepping through the gates.

What is the age restriction for entry?

ECHO VOID is strictly an 18+ event. A valid physical ID (KTP / Driver's License / Passport) is required for wristband exchange. No digital photos of IDs allowed.

What is the dress code?

Underground rave, all-black cyberpunk, or expressive Y2K festival aesthetic. No flip-flops or sports jerseys for security and dancefloor safety.

What items are prohibited?

Illegal substances/drugs (Zero tolerance policy), weapons, liquids from outside, professional DSLRs without media credentials, and laser pointers.
