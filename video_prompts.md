# Video Generation Prompts — The State of Clamour

Dokumentasi prompt video untuk aset visual dan transisi sinematik The State of Clamour.

## 1. Dark Cinematic Transition (Entrance & Veil)

### 🎬 Prompt Utama
```text
Dark cinematic transition. Starting from the gothic tower in the storm (Reference Image 1), the camera dollies forward through the city and toward the ornate entrance. Occasional lightning flashes illuminate the storm clouds in the background. As it approaches the doors, they open to reveal an intense red interior light. The camera continues to move inward, and the scene smoothly transitions into the final frame of absolute darkness with faint red sparks and embers (Reference Image 2). Smooth motion, atmospheric fog, and consistent gothic noir aesthetic. Final state is absolute black with glowing particles.
```

### 📌 Spesifikasi Gerakan & Kamera
* **Shot Type**: Continuous forward dolly zoom / push-in shot
* **Starting Frame**: Gothic tower amidst thunderstorm clouds (Reference Image 1)
* **Mid-Sequence**: Moving through low mist city alley towards ornate grand ceremonial doors, subtle lightning flashes illuminating the sky, doors swing open with crimson illumination spilling outward
* **Ending Frame**: Transition to pure void black screen with subtle drifting crimson sparks / ember particles (Reference Image 2)
* **Pacing**: Steady, fluid cinematic crawl (no sudden cuts or abrupt jerkiness)
* **Atmosphere**: Heavy gothic noir, volumetric dense fog, cold rain mist, dramatic chiaroscuro contrast, glowing red ambient interior glow

### 💡 Rekomendasi Parameter AI Video (Runway Gen-3 / Kling / Luma / Haiper)
* **Motion**: `4 - 5` (Medium smooth)
* **Camera Movement**: `Forward` / `Pan Up subtle`
* **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (Widescreen cinematic) atau `9:16` (Mobile teaser)
* **Negative Prompt**: `bright daylight, cartoon, 3d render look, oversaturated colors, shaky camera, low quality, glitch, morphing artifacts`
